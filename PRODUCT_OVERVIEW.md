# Product Overview

## The Problem

Universities and academic institutions frequently struggle with fragmented communication regarding academic concerns, facility issues, and student feedback.
- Students lack a clear, transparent channel to report issues.
- Class Representatives are overwhelmed with unstructured complaints via WhatsApp or email.
- Administration lacks the analytics to identify systemic issues and track resolution times.

## The Solution

**UniTickets** is a centralized, role-based ticketing system tailored specifically for academic environments. It converts unstructured complaints into manageable, trackable workflows.

By offering a sleek interface for Students to report issues, an organizational dashboard for Class Representatives to assign tasks, and high-level controls for Administration, UniTickets closes the communication gap.

## Target Audience & Roles

1. **Students:** 
   - Experience a simple, frustration-free portal to submit requests.
   - Gain transparency by tracking the status of their reported tickets.
2. **Class Representatives:**
   - Act as the bridge between students and faculty.
   - Utilize Task and Meeting modules to triage tickets and coordinate action plans.
3. **Administration (Admins):**
   - Retain absolute control over user approvals and access.
   - Oversee the entire ecosystem, ensuring accountability across departments.

## Core Modules

- **Ticketing Hub:** The foundational layer where issues are categorized by type (Bug, Academic, Facility) and priority.
- **Task Delegation:** Transforms static tickets into actionable, assignable to-do items with due dates.
- **Meeting Synchronization:** Allows Class Representatives to schedule meetings and automatically link pending tickets as agenda items.
- **User Governance:** A robust admin panel designed to audit, promote, and approve/reject system users.

## Monetization & Expansion Potential

While currently structured for a single institution, UniTickets is architecturally positioned to pivot into a multi-tenant SaaS application:
- **White-labeling:** Institutions could pay a subscription to utilize UniTickets on a custom domain with their proprietary branding.
- **Premium Analytics:** Future iterations could introduce advanced reporting tools (e.g., predictive resolution models, faculty performance metrics) as a premium tier.
- **Integrations:** Opportunities exist to integrate with Canvas, Blackboard, or Microsoft Teams for expanded utility.
